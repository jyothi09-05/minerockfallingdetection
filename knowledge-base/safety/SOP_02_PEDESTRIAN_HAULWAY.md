# Standard Operating Procedure: Pedestrian & Light Vehicle Interaction on Active Haulways

**Document ID**: SOP-MM-SAF-002  
**Version**: 3.1  
**Category**: Safety & Operations  
**Applies To**: All Mine Personnel, Contractors, Light Vehicle Operators, Heavy Haul Fleet  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs all movements, right-of-way rules, and proximity boundaries for pedestrians and light vehicles (LVs) operating on or adjacent to active heavy haul roads, bench access ramps, and crusher feed pads.

## 2. Mandatory Exclusion & Separation Distances
1. **Pedestrian Exclusion Zone**: No unmounted personnel are permitted within **50 meters** of any operating haul truck or hydraulic excavator without direct two-way radio positive contact.
2. **Overtaking Distance**: Light vehicles must maintain a minimum following distance of **60 meters** behind loaded haul trucks on haul ramps (gradient > 6%).
3. **Crusher & Dump Pocket Buffer**: Pedestrians are strictly prohibited within **30 meters** of the active tip head during tipping operations.
4. **Blind Spot Envelope**: The front-right and rear-wheel envelopes of ultra-class haul trucks (such as the CAT 797F / Komatsu 930E) are classified as **Zero-Visibility Danger Zones**.

## 3. Positive Radio Communication Protocol
- Any light vehicle or pedestrian intending to cross or enter a haulage corridor must broadcast on VHF Channel 4 (Pit Dispatch):
  - *"Dispatch and Unit [Truck_ID], this is LV-[Unit_ID] requesting permission to cross ramp at Bench 1400."*
- Movement is authorized **ONLY** after receiving affirmative verbal confirmation and visual eye/beacon contact from the heavy equipment operator.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Telemetry Requirements
- Class 3 high-visibility reflective apparel with active RF proximity responder tag.
- Personal biometric / distress wearable with auto-fall detection enabled.
- Hard hat with reflective strips and integrated cap lamp.
- Steel-toe metatarsal-guard boots (ASTM F2413 compliant).

## 5. Violation Escalation Matrix
- **Tier 1 (Proximity Warning < 30m without radio contact)**: Immediate safety stand-down and mandatory telemetry audit.
- **Tier 2 (Entering Blind Spot Envelope)**: Site access suspension pending root-cause investigation.
