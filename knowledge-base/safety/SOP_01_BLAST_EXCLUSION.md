# Standard Operating Procedure: Blast Exclusion Zone Management
**Document ID**: SOP-SAF-001  
**Category**: Safety & Explosives Management  
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-15  
**Version**: 3.2  

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory safety perimeters, siren notification sequences, and personnel clearance protocols for open-pit blasting operations at Prometheus Pit #4.

## 2. Mandatory Exclusion Radius
1. **Primary Blast Perimeter**: A minimum radial clearance of **500 meters** must be enforced from the blast pattern centroid for all personnel and light vehicles.
2. **Heavy Machinery Clearance**: Ultra-class haul trucks, shovels, and drill rigs must be evacuated to a minimum distance of **300 meters** behind substantial bench catch berms.
3. **Highwall Crest Buffer**: Personnel must remain at least **50 meters** away from any crest above or adjacent to an active blast block.

## 3. Siren Sequence & Radio Signals
- **T-minus 30 Minutes**: Continuous yellow flashing beacons activated at Pit Entry Gate.
- **T-minus 15 Minutes**: First audible blast siren (three intermittent 10-second blasts).
- **T-minus 5 Minutes**: Final clearance siren (continuous 30-second tone). All perimeter sentry posts confirm zone closure on Radio Channel 4 (Blasting Operations).
- **All-Clear Signal**: One continuous 15-second siren following post-blast fume dissipation and misfire inspection by the certified Blast Engineer.

## 4. Emergency Action on Sentries Breach
If an unauthorized worker or vehicle enters the exclusion perimeter within T-5 minutes, the sentry must transmit "ALL STOP, ABORT BLAST" on Channel 4. The Blaster-in-Charge must immediately disconnect firing leads and lock the shot exploder.
