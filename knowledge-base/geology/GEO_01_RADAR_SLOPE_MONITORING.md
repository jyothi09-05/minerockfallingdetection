# Geotechnical Engineering Standard: Real-Time Radar Slope Stability & Deformation Monitoring

**Document ID**: GEO-MM-GT-001  
**Version**: 4.0  
**Category**: Geology & Geotechnical Engineering  
**Applies To**: Geotechnical Engineers, Pit Superintendents, Surveyors, Dispatch  

---

## 1. Overview
Ground-based Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) and real-time prism monitoring stations continuously assess highwall integrity, bench crest stability, and fault slip velocities across all pit sectors (North Wall, East Cut, South Wall, West Flank).

## 2. Deformation Velocity Trigger Action Response Plan (TARP)

| Alert Level | Deformation Velocity | Factor of Safety (FoS) | Action Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GREEN (Normal)** | $< 2.0\text{ mm/day}$ | $\ge 1.30$ | Routine 12-hour automated radar scan cycle. |
| **YELLOW (Advisory)** | $2.0\text{ to } 5.0\text{ mm/day}$ | $1.15 \text{ to } 1.29$ | Increase radar scan frequency to 15-minute cycles. Restrict heavy haul loading on adjacent crests. |
| **ORANGE (Warning)** | $5.0\text{ to } 15.0\text{ mm/day}$ | $1.00 \text{ to } 1.14$ | Restrict bench access below the sector to essential personnel only. Initiate pore pressure dewatering pumps. |
| **RED (Critical Evacuation)** | $> 15.0\text{ mm/day}$ or accelerating | $< 1.00$ | **IMMEDIATE EVACUATION** of the entire sector and all benches below. Dispatch broadcast Level 3 alarm. |

## 3. Structural Geology & Failure Modes
1. **Planar Slip**: Common along daylighting bedding planes dipping $> 35^\circ$ in the North Wall sedimentary series.
2. **Wedge Failure**: Formed at intersections of joint sets J1 ($045^\circ/65^\circ\text{SE}$) and J2 ($315^\circ/70^\circ\text{SW}$).
3. **Toppling / Ravelling**: Occurs in highly fractured basalt benches subjected to freeze-thaw cycles or blast-induced dynamic micro-fractures.

## 4. Remediation Measures
- Dynamic pre-split blast damping along crest lines.
- Horizontal sub-bench drain hole drilling (minimum 60m depth with PVC slotted casing).
- Cable bolting and shotcrete installation along critical ramp switchbacks.
