# Pit Operations Standard: Traffic Rules, Speed Limits & Circuit Right-of-Way

**Document ID**: OPS-MM-TRF-001  
**Version**: 3.0  
**Category**: Mine Operations & Haulage  
**Applies To**: Pit Dispatchers, Equipment Operators, Road Maintenance Crews  

---

## 1. Speed Limits by Mining Zone & Conditions

| Mining Zone / Area | Dry Weather Speed Limit | Wet / Reduced Visibility Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Haul Ramps (Gradient $> 6\%$)** | $40\text{ km/h}$ loaded / $50\text{ km/h}$ empty | $25\text{ km/h}$ loaded / $30\text{ km/h}$ empty |
| **Pit Floor / Bench Straightaways** | $45\text{ km/h}$ | $30\text{ km/h}$ |
| **Crusher Tip Head / Pocket Approach** | $15\text{ km/h}$ | $10\text{ km/h}$ |
| **Shovel Loading Face Envelope** | $10\text{ km/h}$ | $10\text{ km/h}$ |
| **Workshop & Fuel Bay Apron** | $15\text{ km/h}$ | $10\text{ km/h}$ |

## 2. Order of Right-of-Way
In any intersection or merging corridor, the order of precedence is strictly defined as:
1. **Emergency Response Vehicles (Ambulance, Fire, Rescue)** with active emergency beacons.
2. **Loaded Ultra-Class Haul Trucks** ascending haul ramps.
3. **Empty Ultra-Class Haul Trucks** descending haul ramps.
4. **Water Carts & Road Graders** engaged in active road suppression/maintenance.
5. **Light Vehicles & Supervisory Pickups**.

## 3. Intersection & Overtaking Regulations
- Left-hand drive standard is enforced throughout all pit ramps to maximize line-of-sight against highwall safety berms.
- Overtaking heavy haul trucks is strictly prohibited on active haul ramps (gradients $> 4\%$) and within 100 meters of any bench intersection.
- Left turns across opposing traffic lanes require positive radio clearance on VHF Channel 4.

## 4. Safety Berm Specifications
- Safety berm height must equal at least **half the diameter of the largest tire** operating on the road (e.g., minimum $2.0\text{ meters}$ for 59/80R63 equipped haul trucks).
