# Emergency Response Plan: Severe Weather & Pit Inundation / Sump Flash Flood Evacuation

**Document ID**: EMERG-MM-EVAC-001  
**Version**: 2.0  
**Category**: Emergency Response & Disaster Management  
**Applies To**: Incident Commander, Emergency Response Team (ERT), All Mine Personnel  

---

## 1. Trigger Conditions
- **Rainfall Intensity**: $> 25\text{ mm/hour}$ continuous for $> 30\text{ minutes}$, or $> 50\text{ mm}$ cumulative in 2 hours.
- **Pit Sump Water Inflow Rate**: Exceeding active dewatering pump capacity ($> 1,200\text{ m}^3/\text{hour}$).
- **Highwall Run-Off Saturation**: Visual or sensor-detected water cascading down upper bench faces.

## 2. Emergency Escalation Stages

### Phase 1: Weather Advisory (Pre-Alert)
- Telemetry radar monitoring activated.
- Dispatch halts haulage to lowermost sump benches (below Bench 1200).
- Auxiliary diesel dewatering booster pumps staged at South Sump.

### Phase 2: Restricted Operations & Selective Staging
- All light vehicles evacuated from pit floor to intermediate staging bench (Bench 1350).
- Haul trucks rerouted exclusively to upper waste dump circuits.

### Phase 3: Total Pit Evacuation (Code RED)
- Siren broadcast activated across pit public address system (Continuous high-low tone for 60 seconds).
- All personnel immediately proceed via primary escape ramp R-01 to Emergency Assembly Area Alpha (Surface Main Gate).
- In the event R-01 is compromised or flooded, personnel must divert to secondary egress ramp R-02 (West Flank).
- Mustering roll-call verified via RFID gate tags and wearable telemetry within 15 minutes of siren trigger.

## 3. Post-Event Re-entry Protocols
- Pit re-entry is prohibited until:
  1. Geotechnical inspection confirms FoS $\ge 1.30$ across all highwalls.
  2. Sump water level is stabilized below safety markers.
  3. Electrical substations and pump skids are inspected and cleared of moisture hazards.
